01 / Why this exists

Correct words can still make bad localization.

This lab reviews meaning, naturalness, tone, terminology, cultural fit and product usability. A response can be grammatically correct and still feel wrong to a Brazilian user.

04 / Workflow

How the review runs.

STEP A

Design

Build test prompts around translation, product copy, support and locale behavior.

STEP B

Review

Score each answer using a dedicated localization rubric.

STEP C

Log

Capture defects with severity, evidence and expected correction.

STEP D

Decide

Issue a PT-BR readiness recommendation based on defects and thresholds.
